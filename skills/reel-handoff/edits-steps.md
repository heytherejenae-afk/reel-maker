# Finish your reel in Edits (on your phone)

Status: checked on an Android phone on 23 Nov 2026 (see-through card pictures work as overlays). Menu names change between Edits versions; if a button isn't there, look for one with a similar name in the same spot.

You only need this if you want to change something in Edits. The finished reel (`<reel name>.mp4`) is already complete and ready to post.

**Why Edits is different from CapCut:** Edits can't remove a coloured background (its **Cutouts** button looks for a person, not a colour). So for Edits you get:
- `1-video-with-captions.mp4`: your video with the title and captions already on it.
- One picture per overlay card (`card-1-from-0m03s-to-0m09s.png` and so on). The name says when each card shows. The pictures are see-through around the card, so your video shows through.

## Just add a sound and post
1. Move the finished reel to your phone (Step 1 below).
2. Open **Edits** and tap **+**. Tap your reel, then tap **Next**.
3. Tap **Audio** at the bottom to add a trending sound. Turn the sound's volume down so your voice stays clear.
4. Tap **Export** (top right).

## Step 1: Get the files onto your phone

Tested on Android 24 Nov 2026: downloading straight from the Claude app works. Saving to Google Drive from the phone did not.

1. On your phone, open the **Claude** app and go to the chat where Claude sent your reel.
2. Tap the first file under Claude's message. When it opens, tap **Download** (or the three dots, then **Download**). It saves to your phone's **Downloads** and Edits can see it.
3. Do the same for each of the other files.

## Step 2: Start with your video
1. Open **Edits** and tap **+**.
2. Tap `1-video-with-captions`, then tap **Next**. Your video opens with the timeline underneath.

## Step 3: Add each card (do this once per card)
1. Look at the card's name, for example `card-2-from-0m12s-to-0m18s`. That card shows from 12 seconds to 18 seconds.
2. Slide the timeline until the white line is at the start time (the time shows above the timeline, for example 00:12).
3. Tap an empty part of the timeline so nothing is selected. Tap **Overlay** in the bottom toolbar (swipe the toolbar left if you can't see it).
4. Tap the **Photos** tab and pick the card. It appears on your video.
5. The card should land exactly where it was in the reel. If it looks smaller, put two fingers on it and spread them apart until the picture fills the screen edge to edge.
6. A picture starts out a few seconds long. Tap the card's strip on the timeline, then drag its right end until it reaches the end time (for example 00:18).

## Step 4: Export
Tap **Export** at the top right. The video saves to your phone's gallery.
