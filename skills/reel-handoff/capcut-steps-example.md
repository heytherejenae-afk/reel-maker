# Put the apple reel layers together in CapCut (Android phone)

You only need this if you want to change something in CapCut. The finished reel (`apple-trees-in-pots-v1.mp4`) is already complete and ready to post.

**What the layers are.** Each one is the full length of the reel and starts at 0:00, so you never have to line up times:

1. `1-video.mp4`: your cut video with your voice.
2. `2-title-behind-you-pink.mp4`: the "growing apples in pots" title, with you cut out so it sits behind your head.
3. `3-captions-pink.mp4`: the one-word captions.
4. `4-overlays-pink.mp4`: your 8 Canva overlays (price tag, tips, PLANT card).

The pink is a "colour screen". CapCut removes it so only the text or cards show.

## Step 1: Get the files onto your phone
1. On your phone, open the **Claude** app and go to this thread.
2. Tap each layer file below my message. When it opens, tap **Download** (or the three dots, then **Save**). The file saves to your phone's **Downloads**.

## Step 2: Start the project with your video
1. Open **CapCut** and tap **New project** (the big **+** button).
2. Tap the **Videos** tab at the top and tap `1-video` (it's the newest one in Downloads). A tick appears on it.
3. Tap **Add** at the bottom right. Your video appears in the editor with the timeline underneath.

## Step 3: Add each layer on top (do this 3 times: layers 2, then 3, then 4)
1. Tap an empty grey part of the timeline so nothing is selected. Then tap **Overlay** in the bottom toolbar (scroll the toolbar left if you can't see it).
2. Tap **Add overlay**, pick the layer file (for example `2-title-behind-you-pink`), and tap **Add**.
3. It appears as a small pink box on your video. Put two fingers on it and spread them apart until the pink covers the whole screen, edge to edge.
4. Under the main video, a new strip appears. Press and hold that strip, then drag it left until its left edge lines up with the very start (0:00).
5. With the strip still selected (white outline), scroll the bottom toolbar and tap **Remove BG**, then **Chroma key**. (In some versions **Chroma key** is right in the toolbar.)
6. A small circle appears on the video. Drag the circle onto a pink area.
7. Tap **Intensity** and slide it up until the pink disappears (about 20 to 40). If pink edges are left around the text, also nudge **Shadow** up a little.
8. Tap the **tick** (✓) to finish. The pink is gone and the text or cards sit over your video.

## Step 4: Change what you like
- **Delete a layer:** tap its strip, then tap **Delete** in the toolbar.
- **Move a layer:** tap its strip, then drag it on the video.
- **Change the words:** CapCut can't edit words baked into a layer. Tell me what to change in this thread and I'll send a new layer.

## Step 5: Export
Tap **1080P** at the top right and check **Frame rate: 30**. Then tap the **Export** arrow (↑) at the top right. The finished video saves to your phone's gallery.

*Menu names change between CapCut versions. If a button in these steps isn't there, look for one with a similar name in the same spot.*

## On a computer instead
Use the `layers-for-computer` files. The `.mov` layers have see-through backgrounds, so skip the chroma key steps. If CapCut on the computer shows them with a black background, use the pink ones and do Step 3 parts 5 to 8.
