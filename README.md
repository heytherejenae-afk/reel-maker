# Reel Creator

Make Instagram reels and TikToks from idea to finished video, by tapping, not typing prompts.

1. **Ideas**: finds reels that are doing far better than usual in your niche (vidIQ) and turns them into 3 reel ideas with hooks.
2. **Transcribe**: reads what those winning reels say and show (Eden, or the free Get Transcript backup).
3. **Script**: your script page, with what to say, what shows on screen, a shot list and a caption. You can edit it by tapping.
4. **Brand**: your colours and fonts, saved once and used for every reel.
5. **Overlays**: one on-screen card per beat, made in Canva or with 6 built-in styles.
6. **First edit**: cuts your Google Drive clips to the script, with one-word captions, a title behind your head and your overlays.
7. **Hand-off**: the finished MP4, plus layers you can stack in CapCut or Instagram Edits.

**Setup:** follow [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md). It lists every connector, what each one costs, and every tap.

**Start:** in your Reel creator project, say **Make a reel**.
